# Plain language

1. **One idea per sentence.** Aim for 15–20 words; split anything over 30.
2. **Active voice.** "The team migrated the database", not "The database was migrated".
3. **Concrete over abstract.** "cuts query time from 2 s to 200 ms", not "significantly improves performance".
4. **Define jargon once** in parentheses, or replace it. Expand acronyms on first use.
5. **Front-load** paragraphs: the first sentence says what the paragraph is about.
6. **Scannable**: bullets for lists of 3+, tables for comparisons, bold for the few words a skimmer must see.
7. **Cut hedges** ("very", "quite", "it should be noted that") unless the uncertainty is real — then state it precisely.
8. **Keep precision**: plain does not mean vague. Never drop units, numbers, conditions or citations to simplify.
